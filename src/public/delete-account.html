Humaeli account support

Request account and data deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your Humaeli account and the personal data associated with it. You do not need to be signed in to submit a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Select the button below to open an email to our support team.
  2. Include the email address or phone number used for your account.
  3. Send the request from your registered email address when possible.
Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email app, send your request to support@humaeli.com with the subject “Humaeli account and data deletion request”.

Data covered by the request

After we verify account ownership, the request covers the account profile and associated personal data, including chat and AI-chat history, appointments, call records, uploaded profile information, location data, notifications, and active login sessions.

Some transaction, payment, fraud-prevention, or legal records may be retained only when required by applicable law. Those records are restricted and removed when the required retention period ends.

Identity verification

To protect accounts from unauthorized deletion, support may ask you to verify control of the registered email address or phone number. Never include your password or a one-time password in the request.
