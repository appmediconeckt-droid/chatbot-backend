Humaeli iOS Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Who we are and contact

Humaeli Mental Wellness is the app brand operated by MindCrawller Global Private Limited. Contact support@humaeli.com for privacy, correction, access and deletion requests. Website: https://humaeli.com.

Information collected

For new accounts, registration records include the declared eligibility, accepted Terms version and content hash, acceptance time and registration method. Account details include name, anonymous handle, email, phone number, sign-in identifiers, age, gender, profile photo and optional address. Professional accounts may provide qualifications, certificates, specialization and availability. We process text messages, user-selected attachments, wellness information, appointments, consultation notes and prescriptions where used. Historical call, location and financial records may remain subject to retention. Camera and microphone are requested only for an audio or video consultation you choose. Approximate foreground location is collected only after Update Location; the resulting coarse coordinates, address, IP address and location history are stored separately from your manual address. Notifications are requested only after Enable Notifications. Denying access leaves authentication and unrelated features available. QR scanning and biometric App Lock are unavailable. You can select individual photos using the system photo picker without granting library access. The in-app inbox uses authenticated network updates. Manually entered profile and professional details are processed as indicated by their forms. Historical media, location and provider records remain subject to the retention and deletion policy. Authentication uses secure device Keychain storage and server-side sessions. Apple refresh tokens, where configured, are encrypted on the server to support revocation on account deletion.

Purposes

We use this information for registration and authentication, account security, conversations, counselor discovery, consultation and appointment management, in-app notifications, customer support and legal or financial record keeping.

Other service providers and sharing

MongoDB stores backend account and service records; Railway hosts the configured production API. Cloudinary processes public professional profile photos and legacy hosted uploads. New private chat attachments, professional documents and prescription assets are stored in MongoDB and delivered through authenticated endpoints or short-lived download links. Legacy externally hosted private files still require an approved migration; changing app URLs does not erase existing provider copies. Stream processes live consultation media and call metadata. Firebase/APNs process push registration and delivery when enabled. Provider retention and historical copies remain subject to their service practices and cleanup requirements. The current iOS app has no Wallet or Earnings screens or payment step. All appointment bookings are free, including test accounts, and booking creates no payment or earning record. Chat is free for ordinary accounts. No payment gateway is enabled. Legacy test-client simulations outside the mobile booking flow are restricted to designated test accounts and have no monetary value. No actual payment, bank transfer, collection, payout or withdrawal takes place. Bank or card details are not requested. Demo balances cannot be redeemed for real money. Existing historical records, if any, are retained separately from demo records. Google and Apple process sign-in when selected. Configured email/SMS services include Brevo, Resend, SMTP and Twilio; deployment determines which delivery providers are active. Translation features use configured translation providers, including Azure, Google and MyMemory fallback services. Messages may be shared with the counselor you contact, and information needed for support, security or legal obligations may be disclosed for those purposes. No advertising SDK or cross-app advertising tracking was identified in this source audit. For information about provider practices or to make a privacy request, contact support@humaeli.com.

Conversation safety

You can report inappropriate content and block an account from a conversation. A report stores the reporting and reported account identifiers, the conversation reference, selected reason, optional details, timestamps and review status. Reports are available only to authorized moderation accounts. Block settings prevent new messages and bookings between the accounts. Safety reports and block records have no automatic expiry. Their deletion follows the account-data erasure process described below. Contact support@humaeli.com for concerns or requests.

Data Retention and Deletion

While your account is active, we keep account and profile information, conversations, selected attachments, appointments, historical call records and service records to provide your account history and services. Existing expiry rules vary by record type.

To delete your account in the app, open Settings and select Delete Account, then confirm. This option is available to Users and Consultants. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or server records.

After confirmation, account access and active sessions are revoked and live database erasure is attempted during the request, without a waiting period. This removes the account, related conversations, appointments, private files and associated account records. A known active legal hold prevents erasure. Processing or provider-authorization failures are retried; an accepted request alone is not confirmation of deletion.

The app confirms account deletion only after live database erasure is verified. Third-party provider copies, legacy uploads and backups are tracked separately and may need additional processing. Until their cleanup is verified, the app reports that cleanup confirmation is pending. Apple sign-in authorization is revoked where applicable. We keep a limited deletion-job record and cleanup references to process outstanding work and confirm completion; these are not an active account.

Safety reports and block records have no automatic expiry while an account is active and are included in related account-data erasure. Known legal holds can delay erasure. Contact support@humaeli.com for deletion progress, completion confirmation, access, correction or help if you cannot sign in.

Security and your choices

The app communicates with the backend using HTTPS. Authentication uses signed tokens and active sessions; passwords are hashed. Session credentials use device-only Keychain storage without biometric prompts. These controls do not provide end-to-end encryption of stored messages. Optional camera, microphone, foreground location and notification access is requested only when you choose the corresponding feature. Device permission choices can be changed in Settings. The app does not request background location, contacts, Bluetooth, tracking or biometric access. Selecting an individual photo or document shares only the selected file. Removing a device permission or uninstalling the app does not erase historical server records.

Changes

We update this policy when data practices change. Contact support@humaeli.com with privacy questions.
