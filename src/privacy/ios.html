Humaeli iOS Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Who we are and contact

Humaeli Mental Wellness is the app brand operated by MindCrawller Global Private Limited. Contact support@humaeli.com for privacy, correction, access and deletion requests. Website: https://humaeli.com.

Information collected

Account details include name, anonymous handle, email, phone number, sign-in identifiers, age, gender, profile photo and optional address. Professional accounts may provide qualifications, certificates, specialization and availability. We process text messages, user-selected attachments, wellness information, appointments, consultation notes and prescriptions where used. Historical call, location and financial records may remain subject to retention. This iOS release does not request camera, microphone, photo-library, location, notification or biometric access. Audio/video calls, QR scanning, GPS updates, push delivery and biometric App Lock are unavailable. You can select individual photos using the system photo picker without granting library access. The in-app inbox uses authenticated network updates. Manually entered profile and professional details are processed as indicated by their forms. Historical media, location and provider records remain subject to the retention and deletion policy. Authentication uses secure device Keychain storage and server-side sessions. Apple refresh tokens, where configured, are encrypted on the server to support revocation on account deletion.

Purposes

We use this information for registration and authentication, account security, conversations, counselor discovery, consultation and appointment management, calls, notifications, customer support and legal or financial record keeping.

Other service providers and sharing

MongoDB stores backend account and service records; Railway hosts the configured production API. Cloudinary processes public professional profile photos and legacy hosted uploads. New private chat attachments, professional documents and prescription assets are stored in MongoDB and delivered through authenticated endpoints or short-lived download links. Legacy externally hosted private files still require an approved migration; changing app URLs does not erase existing provider copies. Stream and Firebase/APNs may retain historical call or push records. This iOS release has no live calls or push registration. Historical provider copies require cleanup. The current iOS app has no Wallet or Earnings screens or payment step. All appointment bookings are free, including test accounts, and booking creates no payment or earning record. Chat is free for ordinary accounts. No payment gateway is enabled. Legacy test-client simulations outside the mobile booking flow are restricted to designated test accounts and have no monetary value. No actual payment, bank transfer, collection, payout or withdrawal takes place. Bank or card details are not requested. Demo balances cannot be redeemed for real money. Existing historical records, if any, are retained separately from demo records. Google and Apple process sign-in when selected. Configured email/SMS services include Brevo, Resend, SMTP and Twilio; deployment determines which delivery providers are active. Translation features use configured translation providers, including Azure, Google and MyMemory fallback services. Messages may be shared with the counselor you contact, and information needed for support, security or legal obligations may be disclosed for those purposes. No advertising SDK or cross-app advertising tracking was identified in this source audit. For information about provider practices or to make a privacy request, contact support@humaeli.com.

Conversation safety

You can report inappropriate content and block an account from a conversation. A report stores the reporting and reported account identifiers, the conversation reference, selected reason, optional details, timestamps and review status. Reports are available only to authorized moderation accounts. Block settings prevent new messages, call requests and bookings between the accounts. End an existing call with its call controls. Safety reports and block records have no automatic expiry. Their deletion is subject to the retention approval described below. Contact support@humaeli.com for concerns or requests.

Retention and deletion

Consultant chats, call records, appointments and service records support ongoing service history; existing expiry rules vary by record type. A recorded account deletion request disables access and is processed by a durable worker. Apple authorization revocation, where applicable, is retried if Apple is unavailable. An accepted request is not confirmation of completed deletion. Account, clinical, financial and related records are retained for 90 days from the account deletion request. Access is disabled immediately. After 90 days the worker schedules account-data erasure; active legal holds prevent erasure, and provider and backup cleanup must be verified before deletion is confirmed. A 90-day retention period is not a promise that unresolved provider or legal-hold work is complete. Contact support@humaeli.com for progress and completion confirmation. Contact support@humaeli.com for request status, access or correction.

Security and your choices

The app communicates with the backend using HTTPS. Authentication uses signed tokens and active sessions; passwords are hashed. Session credentials use device-only Keychain storage without biometric prompts. These controls do not provide end-to-end encryption of stored messages. This iOS release does not ask for camera, microphone, library, GPS, notification or biometric permissions. Selecting an individual photo or document shares only the selected file. Removing a device permission or uninstalling the app does not erase historical server records.

Changes

We update this policy when data practices change. Contact support@humaeli.com with privacy questions.
