# Humaeli Privacy Policy
Effective date: 20 October 2026

## Who we are and contact
Humaeli Mental Wellness is the app brand operated by MindCrawller Global Private Limited. Contact support@humaeli.com for privacy, correction, access and deletion requests. Website: https://humaeli.com.

## Information collected
Account details include name, anonymous handle, email, phone number, sign-in identifiers, age, gender, profile photo and optional address. Professional accounts may provide qualifications, certificates, specialization, availability and payout details. We process messages and attachments, wellness information you enter, mood and safety context, consultation and appointment information, prescriptions where used, call/session metadata, wallet balances and transaction records. Optional location sharing can include GPS coordinates, city, address, IP address and location history. Push notifications use device platform and notification tokens. Authentication uses session identifiers and server-side token records. You may submit images, documents and voice content through supported features. We do not collect device Face ID templates through the app lock; biometric authentication is handled by your device.

## Purposes
We use this information for registration and authentication, account security, conversations, AI responses, counselor discovery, consultation and appointment management, calls, payments and payouts, notifications, customer support and legal or financial record keeping. AI support is general wellness information, not a diagnosis or emergency service.

## AI Assistant and OpenAI Data Processing
Humaeli uses the OpenAI API, provided by OpenAI, to generate AI Assistant responses. Before AI processing, we request explicit permission in AI Assistant Privacy & Data Sharing. The checkbox is initially unchecked and acceptance is saved against the authenticated account and disclosure version. Declining does not prevent use of unrelated features.

Text messages and up to 20 previous conversation messages pass through Humaeli’s backend to OpenAI. Messages can include personal or mental-wellness information you choose to share. Language, derived mood and safety context, anonymous handle, age, gender, city/location, surroundings and company may accompany chat requests. Selected counselor directory details (name, gender, specialization, experience, languages, consultation mode, rating, match scores and matched topic labels) may be included for recommendations. Full medical records, payment information, prescriptions and appointment details are not automatically added to AI prompts.

AI voice calls are established by Humaeli’s backend; microphone audio then travels directly between your device and OpenAI over WebRTC. OpenAI transcribes audio and generates spoken responses. Voice session instructions include your account role and the profile, situation, safety and counselor directory context described above. Device dictation may separately use Apple or Google speech services. Text selected for AI speech generation is sent to OpenAI. For AI avatars, the selected photo or avatar, customization choices and, when enabled, appearance analysis are sent to OpenAI. Ordinary profile photo uploads do not automatically invoke AI.

You can withdraw consent under Privacy → AI Privacy & Data Sharing → Withdraw AI Consent. The server disables future requests and attempts to terminate registered active AI calls, confirming success only when termination completes. If confirmation fails, retry. Withdrawal does not automatically erase previously processed information. Material disclosure changes require renewed acceptance.

OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default unless the API customer opts in. Abuse-monitoring data may be retained for up to 30 days, with exceptions for legal obligations and safety. Flagged image inputs may be retained for safety review. Retention depends on endpoint and account controls. Humaeli requests store:false for supported chat completions; this does not mean zero retention. Humaeli does not intentionally create stored OpenAI conversations or file-library objects for these features. See https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data for provider-specific details.

## Other service providers and sharing
MongoDB stores backend account and service records; Railway hosts the configured production API. Cloudinary processes hosted uploads. Stream provides calling services; Firebase/Apple push notification services process notification delivery data. Razorpay processes payment transactions and payment details submitted to its payment interface. Google and Apple process sign-in when selected. Configured email/SMS services include Brevo, Resend, SMTP and Twilio; deployment determines which delivery providers are active. Translation features use configured translation providers, including Azure, Google and MyMemory fallback services. Messages may be shared with the counselor you contact, and information needed for support, security or legal obligations may be disclosed for those purposes. No advertising SDK or cross-app advertising tracking was identified in this source audit. For information about provider practices or to make a privacy request, contact support@humaeli.com.

## Conversation safety
You can report inappropriate content and block an account from a conversation. A report stores the reporting and reported account identifiers, the conversation reference, selected reason, optional details, timestamps and review status. Reports are available only to authorized moderation accounts. Block settings prevent new messages, call requests and bookings between the accounts. End an existing call with its call controls. Safety reports and block records have no automatic expiry and are removed when a linked account is deleted. Contact support@humaeli.com for concerns or requests.

## Retention and deletion
Humaeli’s AI chat collection has no automatic expiry. AI messages and responses remain until deleted using Fresh Chat / chat deletion or account deletion. Consultant chats, call records, appointments and service records support ongoing service history; expiry and financial retention vary by record type. Account deletion removes associated account and service records, including AI chats and consent records. Payment history is preserved. Media deletion is attempted for stored Cloudinary profile/certification identifiers; other attachment cleanup, provider copies and infrastructure backups may require a support request. We do not promise immediate deletion from all backups or third-party systems. Contact support@humaeli.com to request access, correction or further deletion. Financial records may need to be retained for applicable accounting, dispute or legal obligations.

Consent state records store provider, version, server timestamps, account reference and up to 100 consent/revocation events, without chat contents or API keys. Active AI call identifiers are retained for termination. Withdrawing consent is separate from deleting history or your account. OpenAI retention is separate from Humaeli’s database retention.

## Security and your choices
The production app communicates with the backend using HTTPS. Authentication uses signed tokens and active sessions; protected AI requests require current server-side consent. Provider credentials remain on the backend in environment configuration. Passwords are hashed by the authentication system. These measures do not constitute end-to-end encryption of stored messages. Camera, microphone, photo library, speech recognition, location and notification access are controlled separately through device settings. Native permission does not count as AI data-sharing consent. Unrelated app features remain available when AI consent is declined.

## Changes
We update this policy when data practices change. Material changes to AI sharing require a new consent version. Contact support@humaeli.com with privacy questions.
