# Humaeli Privacy Policy
Effective date: 17 November 2026

## Who we are and contact
Humaeli Mental Wellness is the app brand operated by MindCrawller Global Private Limited. Contact support@humaeli.com for privacy, correction, access and deletion requests. Website: https://humaeli.com.

## Information collected
Account details include name, anonymous handle, email, phone number, sign-in identifiers, age, gender, profile photo and optional address. Professional accounts may provide qualifications, certificates, specialization, availability. We process messages and attachments, wellness information you enter, mood and safety context, consultation and appointment information, prescriptions where used, call/session metadata, historical wallet balances and transaction records. On iOS, Humaeli requests microphone access for audio/video consultations and camera access for video consultations or a QR scanner action you choose. You can select photos for your profile or chat attachments using the system picker. User accounts in this iOS app do not request device location access. Location is optional for Consultants: only a Consultant tapping Update Location in Settings requests foreground access and shares a one-time location with our server to update your account location. Coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim for reverse geocoding. The account stores coordinates, resolved city/address, request IP and up to 20 history entries. Supported conversation participants and authorized administrators can access stored location information. Revoking location permission does not erase existing history. Startup, login, signup and OTP do not request location, and we do not request Always or background location access. Opening Notifications can request notification access; with authorization, Firebase/APNs registration tokens are sent to our server for push delivery. App Lock uses device authentication only when enabled. Manually entered city, state and address remain optional profile or professional details as indicated by their forms. Previously stored location history, images, notification tokens and service records remain subject to the retention and deletion limitations below. Account information and text conversations are still processed to provide the remaining services. Authentication uses session identifiers and server-side token records. Where configured, Sign in with Apple refresh tokens are stored encrypted on the server so Apple authorization can be revoked when an Apple-linked account is deleted. You may submit images and documents through supported features and share live audio/video during consultations. We do not collect device Face ID templates through the app lock; biometric authentication is handled by your device.

## Purposes
We use this information for registration and authentication, account security, conversations, AI responses, counselor discovery, consultation and appointment management, calls, demo payment simulations, notifications, customer support and legal or financial record keeping. AI support is general wellness information, not a diagnosis or emergency service.

## AI Assistant and OpenAI Data Processing
The current iOS release does not expose AI Chat, AI calls, AI avatars or AI consent controls. The following AI disclosure and navigation apply to other supported platform clients, not this iOS release. Backend AI services remain available to those clients.

Humaeli uses the OpenAI API, provided by OpenAI, to generate AI Assistant responses. Before AI processing, we request explicit permission in AI Assistant Privacy & Data Sharing. The checkbox is initially unchecked and acceptance is saved against the authenticated account and disclosure version. Declining does not prevent use of unrelated features.

Text messages and up to 20 previous conversation messages pass through Humaeli’s backend to OpenAI. Messages can include personal or mental-wellness information you choose to share. Language, derived mood and safety context, anonymous handle, age, gender, city/location, surroundings and company may accompany chat requests. Selected counselor directory details (name, gender, specialization, experience, languages, consultation mode, rating, match scores and matched topic labels) may be included for recommendations. Full medical records, payment information, prescriptions and appointment details are not automatically added to AI prompts.

AI voice calls are established by Humaeli’s backend; microphone audio then travels directly between your device and OpenAI over WebRTC. OpenAI transcribes audio and generates spoken responses. Voice session instructions include your account role and the profile, situation, safety and counselor directory context described above. Device dictation may separately use Apple or Google speech services. Text selected for AI speech generation is sent to OpenAI. For AI avatars, the selected photo or avatar, customization choices and, when enabled, appearance analysis are sent to OpenAI. Ordinary profile photo uploads do not automatically invoke AI.

You can withdraw consent under Privacy → AI Privacy & Data Sharing → Withdraw AI Consent. The server disables future requests and attempts to terminate registered active AI calls, confirming success only when termination completes. If confirmation fails, retry. Withdrawal does not automatically erase previously processed information. Material disclosure changes require renewed acceptance.

OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default unless the API customer opts in. Abuse-monitoring data may be retained for up to 30 days, with exceptions for legal obligations and safety. Flagged image inputs may be retained for safety review. Retention depends on endpoint and account controls. Humaeli requests store:false for supported chat completions; this does not mean zero retention. Humaeli does not intentionally create stored OpenAI conversations or file-library objects for these features. See https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data for provider-specific details.

## Other service providers and sharing
MongoDB stores backend account and service records; Railway hosts the configured production API. Cloudinary processes public professional profile photos and legacy hosted uploads. New private chat attachments, professional documents and prescription assets are stored in MongoDB and delivered through authenticated endpoints or short-lived download links. Legacy externally hosted private files still require an approved migration; changing app URLs does not erase existing provider copies. Stream processes live consultation media and call metadata, and Firebase/APNs process notification registration and delivery. Provider retention and records remain subject to their service practices. The current iOS app has no Wallet or Earnings screens or payment step. All appointment bookings are free, including test accounts, and booking creates no payment or earning record. Chat is free for ordinary accounts. No payment gateway is enabled. Legacy test-client simulations outside the mobile booking flow are restricted to designated test accounts and have no monetary value. No actual payment, bank transfer, collection, payout or withdrawal takes place. Bank or card details are not requested. Demo balances cannot be redeemed for real money. Existing historical records, if any, are retained separately from demo records. Google and Apple process sign-in when selected. Configured email/SMS services include Brevo, Resend, SMTP and Twilio; deployment determines which delivery providers are active. Translation features use configured translation providers, including Azure, Google and MyMemory fallback services. Messages may be shared with the counselor you contact, and information needed for support, security or legal obligations may be disclosed for those purposes. No advertising SDK or cross-app advertising tracking was identified in this source audit. For information about provider practices or to make a privacy request, contact support@humaeli.com.

## Conversation safety
You can report inappropriate content and block an account from a conversation. A report stores the reporting and reported account identifiers, the conversation reference, selected reason, optional details, timestamps and review status. Reports are available only to authorized moderation accounts. Block settings prevent new messages, call requests and bookings between the accounts. End an existing call with its call controls. Safety reports and block records have no automatic expiry. Their deletion follows the account-data erasure process described below. Contact support@humaeli.com for concerns or requests.

## Data Retention and Deletion
While your account is active, we keep account and profile information, conversations, selected attachments, appointments, historical call records and service records to provide your account history and services. Existing expiry rules vary by record type.

To delete your account in the app, open Settings and select Delete Account, then confirm. This option is available to Users and Consultants. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or server records.

After confirmation, account access and active sessions are revoked and live database erasure is attempted during the request, without a waiting period. This removes the account, related conversations, appointments, private files and associated account records. A known active legal hold prevents erasure. Processing or provider-authorization failures are retried; an accepted request alone is not confirmation of deletion.

The app confirms account deletion only after live database erasure is verified. Third-party provider copies, legacy uploads and backups are tracked separately and may need additional processing. Until their cleanup is verified, the app reports that cleanup confirmation is pending. Apple sign-in authorization is revoked where applicable. We keep a limited deletion-job record and cleanup references to process outstanding work and confirm completion; these are not an active account.

Safety reports and block records have no automatic expiry while an account is active and are included in related account-data erasure. Known legal holds can delay erasure. Contact support@humaeli.com for deletion progress, completion confirmation, access, correction or help if you cannot sign in.

## Security and your choices
The production app communicates with the backend using HTTPS. Authentication uses signed tokens and active sessions; protected AI requests require current server-side consent. Provider credentials remain on the backend in environment configuration. Passwords are hashed by the authentication system. These measures do not constitute end-to-end encryption of stored messages. Optional iOS device permissions are requested in the relevant feature. You can decline location and notifications and continue to use other features; audio/video consultations require the corresponding media access. Previously granted access can also be revoked in device settings. Existing backend records are not erased merely by revoking device permissions. Native permission does not count as AI data-sharing consent. Unrelated app features remain available when AI consent is declined.

## Changes
We update this policy when data practices change. Material changes to AI sharing require a new consent version. Contact support@humaeli.com with privacy questions.
